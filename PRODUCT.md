# Talent Studio Product Positioning

## Mission
Help job seekers make better career moves with less wasted effort. Taco uses their real experience to find stronger-fit roles, prepare truthful application work, and keep every external action under their control.

## Vision
Become the trusted, mobile-first career operating system for serious job seekers: one place to build career evidence, choose the right roles, prepare applications, practise interviews, and learn from outcomes.

## Company Objective
Make resume upload the start of a complete career workflow. Within 10 minutes, an activated user should receive at least 3 explainable job matches and open one review-ready application packet. The 12-month product target is 70% useful-match precision at the top 3 recommendations, 80% direct or medium-to-high-confidence job sources, 8% to 12% activated-user conversion, and zero invented protected resume facts in audited outputs.

## Product Purpose
Talent Studio is an AI career workspace for modern job seekers. It helps users build stronger resumes, tailor applications, polish career writing, track opportunities, prepare for interviews, and keep momentum with Taco.

## Primary Audience
- Job seekers applying to competitive roles who need sharper resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn copy, and interview stories.
- Career switchers translating scattered experience into a credible story.
- Professionals who use AI drafts but want their final career writing to sound specific, accurate, and human.

## Positioning
Talent Studio is not a loose collection of AI career tools. The product promise is a Taco-led workflow: upload a resume, define the next move, receive explainable job picks, prepare truthful application materials, review every action, track outcomes, and practise for the interview. Writing polish remains a useful acquisition tool, but it is not the company story.

## Landing Page Register
The root landing page is a brand surface with a live product experience. It should feel like a premium career command center: useful before signup, visually memorable, and broad enough to make the company feel like a career platform.

## Voice
Confident, practical, and trust-first. Avoid guarantees about jobs, interviews, detector bypasses, or safe submission. Preserve user facts and help them communicate real experience more clearly.

## Product Principles
- Deliver value before signup.
- Use free tools as repeatable acquisition hooks.
- Convert through saved history, exports, larger limits, Taco context, and full workflow continuity.
- Make resume-led job matching the main activation path.
- Make the Humanizer a useful side door, not the whole product.
- Keep career outcomes concrete: evidence, fit, follow-up, preparation, and momentum.
- Prefer fewer explainable recommendations over high-volume auto-apply.
- Treat mobile as a complete product surface, not a reduced desktop view.
- Measure product effectiveness, not the number of AI generations.
