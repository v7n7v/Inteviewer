# Visual design brief for Resume Studio concepts

Date: 2026-07-24

## Product surface

Desktop SaaS Resume Studio for TalentConsulting.io.

## Intended user

A job seeker tailoring a trusted master resume to a specific role without fabricated facts.

## Primary goal

Create and review a role-ready resume quickly while understanding exactly what Taco changed and what remains protected.

## Preserve

- Truth guardrails and source verification
- Morph strength
- Live resume preview
- Version history and reversibility
- Export integrity
- Handoff to applications, cover letters, LinkedIn, and interview prep

## Correct

- excessive page height;
- card soup and repeated explanation;
- weak primary-action hierarchy;
- diagnostic dashboards in the core path;
- text-only template overload;
- ambiguous recommendation controls;
- premature downstream actions.

## Experience principles

- One mission, one canvas
- One primary decision per stage
- Trust is a system state, not a card
- Show the document while the user makes consequential choices
- Hide complexity until it becomes relevant
- Make every AI change explainable and reversible

## Concept exploration frame

The three visual concepts should explore genuinely different interaction structures for the Review stage, because that is where morph strength, truth protection, evidence, editing, and document preview converge.

All concepts should:

- fit a 1440 × 1024 desktop canvas;
- use a sophisticated dark TalentConsulting visual language;
- avoid gradients, nested cards, and decorative dashboards;
- use one clear primary action and at most two supporting actions;
- use realistic but fictional resume content;
- show readable, codeable UI rather than a marketing poster;
- preserve a compact workflow progress indicator, Truth Lock, morph strength, a reviewable change, and a live resume preview.
