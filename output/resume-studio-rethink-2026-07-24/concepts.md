## 1. Focused Workbench

![Focused Workbench](./concept-focused-workbench-1440x1024.png)

### Interaction model

A compact left workflow rail, central change-review queue, and persistent right document preview.

### Best qualities

- balances guidance and control;
- keeps morph strength, diagnostics, provenance, and the document in one view;
- easiest evolution from the current product;
- strong foundation for the rest of the TalentConsulting tool suite.

### Tradeoff

The central queue can become busy if lower-priority details are not aggressively collapsed.

## 2. Guided Narrative

![Guided Narrative](./concept-guided-narrative-1440x1024.png)

### Interaction model

The resume is the main canvas. The user reviews one change at a time, with chapter progress on the left and evidence on the right.

### Best qualities

- calmest and most intuitive direction;
- strongest reduction in cognitive load;
- makes trust and source evidence easy to understand;
- excellent for first-time and non-technical users.

### Tradeoff

Power users may want a faster multi-change overview or batch-review mode.

## 3. Diff Studio

![Diff Studio](./concept-diff-studio-1440x1024.png)

### Interaction model

Source and tailored resumes stay synchronized side by side, with a precise change inspector.

### Best qualities

- clearest proof that Taco is not inventing facts;
- most powerful and transparent review model;
- highly differentiated from generic resume builders;
- excellent for expert users and consequential applications.

### Tradeoff

Side-by-side comparison needs a simplified tablet/mobile mode and careful control of text density.

## Suggested synthesis

If a hybrid direction is chosen later, the strongest combination is:

- Guided Narrative as the default review experience;
- Focused Workbench as the overall Studio shell;
- Diff Studio as an optional “Compare source” mode for power users.
